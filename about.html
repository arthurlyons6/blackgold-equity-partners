About / The firm

Private equity, run as an operating system.

Black Gold is an operating layer that acquires, transforms and exits businesses, with measurable outcomes at every milestone.

Mission

Lower the integration risk.

Acquisitions are won or lost after close, and the first 100 days shape the exit. We replace tribal knowledge, handoffs and static models with a documented operating system on day one. Every decision, metric and deadline lives in the environment the owners use to run the business.

Approach

Operational control, term sheet to exit.

The system standardizes research, diligence, integration, reporting and exit planning. Decisions get faster, governance gets tighter, and management, the board and investors see the same live record of how value is created.

Middle-market returns come from execution quality more than leverage multiples. We turn execution into infrastructure.

Audience

Built for owners choosing a partner, institutional investors who need transparent reporting, and advisors who want repeatable execution.
