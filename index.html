One system from first call to exit.

Research, outreach, diligence, integration, portfolio operations and exit planning run in one environment. Every deal, business and workflow is measured inside it, with KPIs at every step.

  1. Research & sourcing

    Market maps and target scoring surface the right deals, so partners spend their time on execution.
