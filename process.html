Process / Sequence

From first conversation to close.

Every engagement follows the same operating sequence, with predictability, visibility and governance built in from the first meeting.

Sequence

A disciplined operating rhythm.

Five stages, each with a named agent that keeps the work moving and the record complete.

  1. First conversation

    Understand the business, its goals and what success looks like before structure or terms come up.

    Agent: Scout

  2. Due diligence

    Operational, financial and cultural assessment mapped against the BOS readiness framework.

    Agent: Analyst

  3. Acquisition & integration

    Close, install dashboards, align operations and start the first 100-day transformation within weeks.

    Agent: Operator

  4. Build & exit

    Hit 100-day KPI gates, reduce risk and position for a clean institutional exit with transparent reporting.

    Agent: Strategist

  5. Outcomes

    KPI gates, reporting cycles and integration milestones are enforced by the system, with owners, dates and success criteria in view.

    Agent: Auditor

Outcomes

Milestones you can measure.

Integration milestones, KPI gates and reporting cycles are enforced through the system. Owners, dates and success criteria stay visible to management, the board and investors.
